# Phase 1 – Individual Problem Scan

## Objective

The purpose of this phase is to identify real-world problems through personal observation before proposing any AI-based solutions. The focus is on understanding user pain points, repetitive tasks, and opportunities where AI can provide meaningful value.

---

# Problem 1: Missing Important Deadlines

## Problem Statement

Students and working professionals often miss assignment deadlines, project milestones, or meeting schedules because information is scattered across multiple platforms such as email, LMS (Moodle), messaging applications, and calendars.

### Actor
- Students
- Working professionals

### Repetitive Tasks
- Checking emails, LMS, calendars, and messaging apps daily.
- Tracking newly assigned tasks and deadlines.
- Recording deadlines manually.

### Time-Consuming
- Spending 10–20 minutes every day checking multiple platforms.
- Prioritizing tasks manually.
- Handling urgent work after missing deadlines.

### AI Advantage
- Aggregate deadlines from multiple sources.
- Prioritize tasks automatically.
- Provide personalized reminders.
- Predict potential overdue tasks.

### User Pain Points
- Missing assignment submissions.
- Increased stress before deadlines.
- Constantly switching between different applications.
- Poor time management.

# Problem 2: Debugging Source Code

## Problem Statement

Debugging software is one of the most time-consuming tasks for developers and computer science students. Identifying the root cause of an error often requires reading logs, searching documentation, and experimenting with multiple solutions.

### Actor
- Computer Science students
- Software developers

### Repetitive Tasks
- Writing code.
- Running programs repeatedly.
- Reading error logs.
- Searching for similar issues online.

### Time-Consuming
- Debugging may take from several minutes to multiple hours.
- Reading long stack traces.
- Testing multiple fixes.

### AI Advantage
- Explain compiler/runtime errors.
- Identify root causes.
- Recommend possible fixes.
- Suggest optimized code.
- Detect potential bugs before execution.

### User Pain Points
- Difficult to understand error messages.
- Slow debugging process.
- Reduced productivity.
- Frustration when solving complex bugs.

# Problem 3: Organizing Exam Preparation Materials

## Problem Statement

Students spend a significant amount of time collecting and organizing study materials before examinations. Learning resources are usually distributed across slides, lecture notes, PDFs, videos, and online documents.

### Actor
- Students

### Repetitive Tasks
- Searching for study materials.
- Reading multiple documents.
- Combining notes from different sources.

### Time-Consuming
- Requires several hours or even days.
- Duplicate information across resources.
- Difficult to identify important topics.

### AI Advantage
- Summarize learning materials.
- Generate concise study notes.
- Create flashcards.
- Generate practice questions.
- Recommend personalized study plans.

### User Pain Points
- Information overload.
- Uncertainty about what to study.
- Repetitive reading.
- Missing important concepts.

# Problem 4: Lack of Time for Regular Exercise

## Problem Statement

Many students and working professionals struggle to maintain a consistent exercise routine because of busy schedules and changing daily commitments.

### Actor
- Working professionals
- Busy students

### Repetitive Tasks
- Balancing study, work, and personal life.
- Postponing exercise repeatedly.
- Planning workout schedules manually.

### Time-Consuming
- Finding available time for exercise.
- Planning workout routines.
- Rebuilding motivation after interruptions.

### AI Advantage
- Analyze personal schedules.
- Recommend suitable workout times.
- Generate personalized exercise plans.
- Track progress.
- Provide intelligent reminders.

### User Pain Points
- Lack of free time.
- Difficulty maintaining healthy habits.
- Low motivation.
- Declining physical health.

# Problem 5: Managing Daily Tasks and Schedules

## Problem Statement

Managing multiple daily tasks across different applications often leads to forgotten responsibilities, scheduling conflicts, and reduced productivity.

### Actor
- Students
- Working professionals

### Repetitive Tasks
- Planning daily schedules.
- Updating task lists.
- Tracking progress.
- Switching between productivity applications.

### Time-Consuming
- Creating schedules manually.
- Reorganizing plans when priorities change.
- Managing multiple productivity tools.

### AI Advantage
- Automatically organize daily schedules.
- Prioritize tasks intelligently.
- Adapt schedules based on changes.
- Estimate task completion time.
- Deliver smart reminders.

### User Pain Points
- Forgetting important tasks.
- Schedule conflicts.
- Difficulty deciding task priorities.
- Feeling overwhelmed by workload.
